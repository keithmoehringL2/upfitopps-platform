Demo the calling session
  1. Open My Queue from the email, or tap a company to deep-link.
  2. Tap Call (doesn't count), then pick an outcome. Save & Next moves to the next prospect.
  3. Confirm a suggested decision-maker; it's tracked separately from reaching them.
  4. Set "2 weeks" as follow-up and watch it move to Upcoming under Follow Up Reminders.
  5. Flag Opportunity identified; the company leaves the cold queue.
  6. Finish the list to reveal Continue Prospecting. Try ZIP 48104.
Rules applied
  • Queue of {{ target }}: {{ splitTop }} highest-priority eligible + {{ splitNew }} never-contacted (rounded), then sorted by score.
  • Default cadences: No Answer 3 days, Voicemail 5, Gatekeeper 7, Decision-Maker 14, Not interested now 60, Wrong Number none (no follow-up).
  • Explicit follow-up dates override cadence and surface under Due today.
  • Customer reminders and follow-ups never count toward the cold goal unless a cold attempt is saved.
  • Skip records nothing. ZIP search never reassigns.
Assumptions and questions for review
  1. Small-queue rounding: never-contacted share = round(target × 0.3). Confirm with engineering.
  2. Wrong Number has no follow-up by default; the company stays out of queues. Should it also flag the record for L2 data correction?
  3. "Not interested now" is a choice under Spoke to Decision-Maker, using the manager's 60-day setting.
  4. Confirming a decision-maker and recording a quote save immediately (audited); outcomes save with Save & Next.
  5. Deal Won is omitted until you decide whether it stays in MVP.
  6. Snooze defaults to 7 days. Is that the right default, and should managers set it?
  7. Skipped prospects stay visible today and return in a later queue under normal ranking.
  8. ptc.upfitopps.com styling wasn't verified; branding runs on the Broadsheet tokens with the distributor color swapped in.

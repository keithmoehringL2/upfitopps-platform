Document purpose: primary functional and technical specification for mockups, implementation planning,
development, and acceptance testing. Specific UI arrangements may be refined during prototyping without
changing the business rules below.
1. Product definition and scope
Upfit Opps is an L2 Digital-owned, white-label, multi-tenant sales execution and accountability application for
commercial vehicle distributors. L2 prepares company-level market data outside the product and uploads ready-
to-use CSV files. Upfit Opps displays that information, assigns accountable owners, delivers prioritized outreach
queues, captures documented outcomes, supports existing-customer touch reminders, and provides
distributor/manufacturer reporting. It is not a CRM, data enrichment engine, quoting system, or sales management
agency.
 Primary benefit to distributor: less prospect research and decision fatigue; more consistent relationship-
building and simple follow-up.
 Primary benefit to manufacturer: regional visibility into outreach, decision-maker engagement, opportunities,
and quoted companies resulting specifically from manufacturer-supplied VIO cohorts.
 Initial target manufacturer: Adrian Steel. Manufacturer model must support additional brands independently.
 MVP does not perform web scraping, VIO consolidation, AI validation, score calculation, CRM/ERP/quoting
integrations, marketing campaigns, or opportunity stage management.

3. Multi-tenant account provisioning and branding
 One shared application with strict tenant isolation in database queries and authorization. Each distributor has
its own brand, data, settings, users, and URL such as truckcolors.upfitopps.com.
 L2 Admin Portal is the sole internal account-creation and maintenance interface; former “L2 Tenant Setup” is
not a separate product.
 Create distributor: account name, unique validated subdomain slug, branding logo and colors, primary
manager username/email, initial sales reps, related manufacturer program, assigned manufacturer regional
representative.
 Wildcard DNS, wildcard HTTPS and application subdomain routing permit provisioning new distributor URLs
without manual DNS entries per distributor, subject to deployment configuration.
 L2 admins can access any distributor administration area and update branding, settings, manager/rep users,
and CSV data.
 Design should mirror the white-label branding pattern of ptc.upfitopps.com/cost-calculator. Exact appearance
has not been visually verified; designers should obtain an approved screenshot/design tokens before high-
fidelity fidelity claims.
4. Authentication and security
 Distributor managers sign in at upfitopps.com with username and password and land on Manager Overview;
use secure password hashing, password recovery and rate limits.
 Sales reps do not use passwords. First access or expired session requires short-lived, single-use email
verification; subsequent daily-email deep links open a secure persistent device session, initially targeting 30-
day renewable validity.
 Daily email links are contextual navigation links, not permanent authentication keys. Manager can revoke rep
access and pause emails.
 Manufacturer regional/leadership users have their own authenticated portal accounts (implementation may
use password-based login); never inherit distributor access.
 Authorize every API call against role, distributor ID, manufacturer ID, program ID, regional assignment and
record source. Session cookies must be Secure/HttpOnly/SameSite and revocable; add audit trails for high-
impact edits.

5. Offline data preparation versus in-product CSV import
OUTSIDE platform (L2 service): obtain VIO vehicle-level records; consolidate into unique companies and vehicle
fleet aggregates; research websites/phones/possible decision-makers; use AI then human exception review for
unverifiable/disagreeing records; preserve VIO primary industry and subcategory and validate both; calculate
priority score; remove/resolve bad records; generate normalized CSV. None of these processes or review queues
belong in the Upfit Opps MVP.
INSIDE platform: L2 admin selects the target distributor account, uploads prepared CSV from desktop,
maps/validates columns, previews add/update counts and errors, then commits an import. Initial and subsequent
CSVs add new company records and update matching existing records.
 Use stable distributor-scoped external company ID as preferred upsert key; reject ambiguous matches for
explicit correction before import rather than silently merging.
 Preview missing required fields, invalid priority scores, unknown owner emails, bad ZIP formatting, duplicate
IDs and conflicting source/program metadata. Keep an import audit log and rollback/recovery mechanism.
 Protect locally edited ownership, confirmed primary decision-maker, activities, opportunity and quote history,
suppression state, and follow-up dates during updates; an explicit authorized field-level override is required to
replace these.
 Company source/program attribution must be immutable enough for defensible manufacturer reporting;
matching an existing customer to VIO data must not expose past customer interactions to that manufacturer.
 Priority score is imported on an approximately 0–5 scale, higher is better. No score calculation/recalculation
happens inside the product. A later CSV may replace the stored value.

Identifying a primary decision-maker is different from speaking with that person. AI-suggested candidates never

7. Ownership and company repository
 CSV import assigns an owner only when an explicit valid owner is provided. Otherwise record remains
unassigned. There are NO ZIP-based or territory-rule automatic assignments.
 Distributor Manager can browse, sort, search and filter all companies by ZIP, priority, industry, source, status,
owner and other relevant fields. Select one, many or all filtered results, then bulk assign, reassign or unassign.
 Daily queues draw from assigned companies only; unassigned companies remain out of daily queues until
manager assigns them. The former “auto-claim first daily delivery” approach is removed.
 Reassignment moves active follow-up visibility and prospective queue eligibility to new owner while keeping
complete historical activities. Track who changed ownership and when. Bulk reassignment supports rep
departure.
 Any rep may access a colleague-owned record and log activity/quote updates; this does not change ownership.
8. Cold outreach eligibility, priority and queue rules
 Eligibility first: company assigned to rep, cold-eligible status, not suppressed, due under outcome cadence or
explicit follow-up, no active opportunity exit, and not already completed for the session.
 Daily recommended count is manager-configured per rep (e.g. 10 or 20), not a call limit. Reps may continue
working as many additional prospects as desired.
 For each recommended cold queue, allocate 70% to highest-priority eligible companies including previously
contacted due accounts, and 30% to eligible NEVER-CONTACTED companies ranked highest first. If either pool
is insufficient, fill from the other. Define deterministic rounding for small queues.
 Specific scheduled follow-up dates override normal outcome cadence and must be surfaced as due reminders
on/after that date; they should not be silently deferred behind lower priority calls.
 Log “Skip” means do not record a call and do not count toward target; skip does not change ownership or
suppress permanently. “Remove from Outreach” keeps company/history but excludes further prospecting
until manually restored. A Do Not Contact suppression is persistent and must not be reactivated by refresh.
 Following a recorded outcome, next eligible date is calculated from distributor manager-configured intervals
(no answer, voicemail, gatekeeper, not interested etc.). Manager settings affect future scheduling, not prior
activity events.
 Daily queue is a stable ordered session for a rep; email link can open whole queue or a specific company. Next
Company moves through list; after initial list is exhausted, Continue Prospecting appends eligible ranked
prospects without a hard ceiling.

list, direct company links, and “Open My Queue”. Existing-customer reminders may appear distinctly. No
leaderboard in emails. Clicking a company navigates to that exact record after session validation.

 Header with rep name, today’s recorded cold attempts versus target and remaining target, then ranked cards
for the manager-configured daily cold prospect count.

 ZIP Code Prospect Finder: type exactly one five-digit ZIP; display cold contacts in that ZIP, sorted by priority,

9.3 Company Detail — ONE main action screen
 Company name, priority, address, website, fleet mix, industry, contact details, clickable telephone and optional
directions link; show current assigned owner and source as appropriate.
 One confirmed Primary Decision-Maker area with ability to select from AI candidate list or manually
enter/update name, title, phone/email and notes.
 Activity summary near top (total recorded attempts, last recorded attempt/outcome, next follow-up), followed
by reverse-chronological Activity Feed (date, user, outcome, note); no separate manual Add Activity tool.
 Log Outcome controls live ON this screen: No Answer, Left Voicemail, Spoke to Gatekeeper, Spoke to Decision-
Maker, Wrong Number; optional notes and follow-up date; an independent Opportunity Identified action/flag
and Remove from Outreach control.
 Outcome can request contact details relevant to gatekeeper/decision-maker conversation; save creates
timestamped activity and updates eligibility/follow-up. Tapping Call alone does not count as outreach.
 Fixed bottom action navigation: Skip / Save & Next Company (or Next Company after saving); direct progress
through queue without reopening email. Prevent double submission and show saved confirmation.
 Reps and managers may record a quote count or quote-issued event against ANY company on the company
detail record. No price field or stage editor.
10. Existing-customer touch-base
L2 imports existing and past customers with independently supplied per-company touch-base interval in days. The
platform is a reminder safety net, not an authoritative log of all incoming calls/quotes; show “last recorded touch,”
never claim last actual conversation. Existing-customer reminders appear separately from cold goals. A rep can
use the standard record outcome action, “Already Contacted” to reset next cadence, or Snooze to defer. Interactions
not entered in Upfit Opps cannot automatically reset the clock. No integrations with distributor ordering/quoting
systems.
11. Opportunity and quote reporting — intentionally minimal
 Rep flags Opportunity Identified from Company Detail; optionally adds description. No qualification threshold,
manager approval, stage definitions or pipeline stages.
 On save, company immediately exits routine cold outreach, preserving owner, source attribution, activity
history, notes and explicit follow-ups; reporting counts a unique company as opportunity-identified.
 Any sales rep or distributor manager can manually enter or increment quote count for any company, with
date and actor audited. No quote matching, automated reconciliation, quote amounts, opportunity values,
revenue, margins or quote contents.
 Report number of unique companies with at least one quote separately from total quote events/count. Optional
Deal Won flag only if retained in MVP without pipeline behavior; do not require deal stages or complex
workflows.

12. Distributor Manager screens and settings
Manager Overview
Default post-login screen: team outreach-to-opportunity funnel and rep performance table (period selectable).
Include recorded attempts, target attainment, identified primary decision-makers, decision-maker conversations,
identified opportunities, companies quoted. Remove separate “untouched prospects” overview module. Upper-
right gear opens Manager Settings.
Manager Settings (single consolidated screen)
 Sales Team: add/edit/pause/disable sales reps, email, per-rep cold target, daily email eligibility, revoke sessions,
bulk reassign departed reps.
 Lead Management: search/filter/sort full repository, inspect details, bulk assign/reassign/unassign, manage
suppressions. May be a section/tab of the same Settings screen rather than a separate navigation surface.
 Outreach: manager-adjustable cadence by call outcome, “not interested now” duration (e.g. 60 days), existing-
customer reminder behavior, rep email schedule; dates explicitly requested by prospect override defaults.
 Reporting: daily or weekly manager summary email, delivery time, recipients, team and per-rep counts/rates
with optional trend comparison; no grade.
 Gamification: optional team leaderboard on/off; available only inside rep mobile dashboard, not outgoing
emails.
13. Manufacturer portal (multi-manufacturer by design)
 L2 creates manufacturers, manufacturer regional reps, and leadership users; associates each distributor’s
specific manufacturer program with its designated regional rep. A distributor may participate in more than
one manufacturer program, with independent permissions and source cohorts.
 Regional rep login shows aggregate performance across only their assigned distributors plus clickable
Distributor Detail reports. Leadership login shows all distributors/regions belonging to THEIR manufacturer,
using same two screens with broader scope.
 Every manufacturer metric is calculated only from source-attributed leads provided under its program AND
outreach/outcomes attributable to that program. Distributor-sourced existing customers and unrelated
activities remain invisible.
 Allowed manufacturer measures: source cohort size, Market Outreach Rate, outreach attempts/ranges,
primary decision-makers confirmed and reached, opportunities identified, companies quoted, conversion
rates and time trends.
 No access to distributor proprietary customer lists, unrelated cold leads, private notes outside permitted
program reporting, quotes/financial amounts, revenues, margins or competitor-sensitive details. Enforce
isolation in server queries and exports, not merely UI filtering.
 If a VIO-sourced prospect later becomes a customer, that status must not grant permanent manufacturer
access to the distributor’s later, unrelated customer relationship or activities.

17. Commercial package and manufacturer pilot (not application features)
 Distributor onboarding: $5,000 one-time, covering external L2 data preparation and human verification
exceptions, initial usable lead dataset, tenant setup, configuration, import and initial training. Scope and
minimum data quality must be defined contractually.
 Subscription: $500/month includes first three active sales reps; $85/month per additional active sales rep.
Initial pricing to be validated after first 5–10 accounts. No advisory services bundled.
 Included benefit: monthly Upfit Opps distributor sales-insights email with anonymized cross-network lessons
and prospecting best practices, without exposing distributor-specific business information.
 L2 Digital advisory, email/content campaigns, CRM work, marketing automation and advertising are separate
optional services with separately quoted fees. Not features of platform MVP.
 Substantial new VIO data preparation / annual refresh may be quoted separately rather than automatically
included in monthly subscription.
 Initial Adrian Steel proposal: free manufacturer portal pilot for approximately 3–6 months in exchange for VIO
cohorts, referrals and active distributor adoption encouragement; later optional negotiated paid manufacturer
enhanced reporting license; proprietary distributor information remains off-limits at all tiers.
 L2 owns software IP, reserves non-exclusive multi-manufacturer rights. Confirm VIO licensing, distributor
source rights, consent and manufacturer report permissions in written agreements.
18. Acceptance scenarios — must pass before pilot launch
 An L2 admin creates a distributor slug, configures logo/colors, manager and reps; the subdomain resolves
correctly and cannot expose a different tenant.
 CSV preview shows create/update/error totals. Reimport updates company fleet/priority/import fields but
preserves owner changes, activities, quote counts and confirmed primary decision-maker unless explicitly
permitted.
 An imported unassigned record does not appear in rep queues; manager filters by ZIP and bulk assigns, after
which it becomes eligible for owner’s queue.
 Rep email opens queue or exact company after verification; persistent session works on repeat visits; revoked
rep access fails.
 Queue with 20 cold prospects starts with 14 highest-priority eligible and 6 never-contacted when both pools
have supply. Never-contacted pool ranked by imported score.
 Tap-to-call without saved outcome does not count as an attempt. Skip does not count. Saved
voicemail/gatekeeper/decision-maker outcomes create correctly timestamped activity and history.
 Rep confirms one candidate as Primary Decision-Maker; suggested alternatives remain visible. Confirmation is
counted separately from actual decision-maker conversation.
 Rep sets explicit follow-up date two weeks ahead; default cadence does not surface company prematurely; due
reminder appears on date.
